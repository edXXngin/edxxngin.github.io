Privacy Policy

Engin Dengiz Games · Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play games published by Engin Dengiz Games (the "Games"), including Yarn Path. Engin Dengiz Games is operated by Engin Dengiz, an individual developer based in Istanbul, Türkiye ("we", "us").

In short: our Games have no accounts and no sign-in, we do not collect your name, email or contacts, and your game progress stays on your device. The Games are free and show ads provided by Google AdMob, which processes some device data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels completed, coins, daily streak, unlocked items) and settings (sound, vibration) are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Deleting the Game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Games display banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (impressions, clicks).

Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized (based on your interests) or non-personalized (based on context only). Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are personalized, the Game asks for your consent using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • iOS: the Game asks for permission to track via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • US state privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the in-game privacy options where shown, and through your device's ad settings described above.

4. Analytics

We may use aggregated, anonymous gameplay statistics (for example, how many players finish level 10) to improve the Games. If an analytics service is added, it will be listed here before it is used.

5. Children

The Games are designed for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Legal basis (EEA/UK) and your rights

Ads are shown on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding free games, and personalized ads only with your consent. Under GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and similar laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through the choices in Section 3 or directly with Google. You may also contact us, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Data retention and security

We do not operate servers that store your data. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new service. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
