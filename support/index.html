Bank Shot — FAQ

How do I play? Drag to aim (the guide shows your first bounce) and release to fire. Break all the glass before you run out of shots.

A level seems impossible. Every level is checked to be solvable. Try a different first bounce, or email us the level number.

Switch Rush — FAQ

How do I play? Tap a switch to flip it and send each train to the station of its colour. Hold anywhere to fast-forward.

The trains are too fast. Use the slow-down option in the level, or replay the level from the level map.

Sneaky Steps — FAQ

How do I play? Tap to start, then tap anywhere to jump. Reach the green door at the bottom of the tower. Every trap has a visible hint.

I'm stuck on a level. After a few falls a Skip button appears. Every level is checked to be beatable.

Orbit Guard — FAQ

How do I play? Slide your finger around the stick to turn the shield. Your cannon fires on its own. After every wave, pick one of three upgrade cards.

I keep losing early. Spend the coins you earn on permanent upgrades on the home screen, then try a new card build.

Tile Fall — FAQ

How do I play? Tap a column to take its bottom tile into the tray. Three matching gems clear. Clear every tile before the seven tray spots fill up.

A level seems impossible. Every level is checked to be solvable. Use undo, or replay the level from the level map.
