Yarn Path — FAQ

How do I play? Start at number 1 and drag one continuous line through every square, passing the numbers in order. Drag back along the line to undo.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device only. Reinstalling the game or clearing its data resets it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open the game's Settings → Privacy choices (shown in regions where it applies), or see our Privacy Policy.

A level seems impossible. Every level has exactly one solution. Try the hint button, or email us the level number.
